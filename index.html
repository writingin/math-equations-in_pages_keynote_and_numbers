A classic example of mathematical typesetting

\[ \displaystyle \int\limits_{-\infty}^{+\infty} \mathrm{e}^{-x^2}\,\mathrm{d}x = \sqrt{\pi} \]

The Gaussian integral is a classical mathematical identity. Notice the upright roman letters e and d, while the variable x remains italic.

\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} \mathrm{e}^{-x^2}\,\mathrm{d}x = \sqrt{\pi}

The equation is rendered using MathJax. This example demonstrates precise mathematical notation and consistent typography.
